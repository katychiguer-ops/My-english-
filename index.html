📚 Grammar — Easy English

No pressure. Learn one small idea at a time.

🔮 Future — WILL

When we talk about the future, we can use will.

I will marry you. 💜
You will travel.
He will work.
She will study.
It will rain.
We will go.
They will come.

⏪ Past

For something that already happened.

I worked yesterday.
She visited Morocco.
They traveled last year.

🌱 Present Simple

For habits and things that happen regularly.

I work every day.
You study English.
He works every day.
She studies English.

💡 With he / she / it, we usually add -s or -es.

🏃 Present Continuous

For something happening now.

I am studying.
She is cooking.
They are traveling.
