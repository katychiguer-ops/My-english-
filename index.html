📚 Easy Grammar

No pressure. Understand first, then practice.

🌱 Present Simple

نستخدمه للعادات، الحقائق، والأشياء التي تحدث بشكل متكرر.

القاعدة:
I / You / We / They + base verb
He / She / It + verb + s/es/ies
I work every day.
You work every day.
She works every day.
He studies English.

⏳ Past Simple

نستخدمه لشيء حدث وانتهى في الماضي.

Regular verbs:
work → worked
play → played
study → studied

Irregular verbs:
go → went
eat → ate
see → saw

🚀 Future with WILL

نستخدم will + base verb للتحدث عن المستقبل.

I will work.
She will travel.
They will study.

🔤 How verbs change

1. Add S

work → works
read → reads
play → plays

2. Add ES

go → goes
watch → watches
wash → washes
fix → fixes

3. Change Y to IES

study → studies
try → tries
carry → carries

⚠️ إذا كان قبل Y حرف متحرك مثل play، لا نحذف Y: play → plays

4. Irregular verbs

بعض الأفعال لا تتبع قاعدة -ed ويجب تعلم شكلها الماضي.
